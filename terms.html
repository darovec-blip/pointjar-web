The service

PointJar is an app for a family points system. We provide it free of charge, with no ads and no in-app purchases. We may keep developing and changing the app.

Account and family

An Apple or Google account is required. An adult creates the family and becomes its admin; everyone else joins with a one-time code valid for 48 hours.

The admin is responsible for whom they invite and for the child’s account. Do not share invite codes with anyone outside the family.

One account can belong to one family at a time.

Using the app

Points, tasks and rewards are set by the family and exist only within it. Points have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money or transferred outside the family.

You must not use the app to harass anyone, upload content you have no rights to, or attempt unauthorised access to other families’ data.

Content you add

Task names, reward names and photos remain yours. You only grant us the right to store them and show them to members of your family so the app can work.

Liability

The app is provided “as is”. We do our best to keep it reliable but do not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation. We are not responsible for family decisions made on the basis of points in the app.

Our liability is limited to the extent permitted by law; we do not exclude liability for intentional damage or gross negligence.

Ending it

You can delete your account at any time under My account. If you are the last admin of a family, the whole family and its history are deleted — this cannot be undone.

If someone seriously breaches these terms, we may restrict or terminate their access.

Governing law and contact

Konto a rodina

Na používanie treba Apple alebo Google konto. Rodinu zakladá dospelý, ktorý sa tým stáva jej správcom (adminom); ostatní vstupujú jednorazovým kódom platným 48 hodín.

Názvy úloh, odmien a fotografie zostávajú vaše. Dávate nám len právo ich uložiť a zobraziť členom vašej rodiny, aby appka fungovala.

Ukončenie

Konto môžeš kedykoľvek zmazať v sekcii Moje konto. Ak si posledný admin rodiny, zmaže sa celá rodina aj s históriou — nedá sa to vrátiť.

Konto und Familie

Für die Nutzung ist ein Apple- oder Google-Konto nötig. Ein Erwachsener gründet die Familie und wird damit ihr Admin; alle anderen treten mit einem Einmal-Code bei, der 48 Stunden gilt.

Der Admin ist dafür verantwortlich, wen er einlädt, und für das Konto des Kindes. Gib Einladungscodes niemandem außerhalb der Familie.

Ein Konto kann gleichzeitig Mitglied einer Familie sein.

Punkte, Aufgaben und Belohnungen legt die Familie selbst fest; sie gelten nur innerhalb der Familie. Punkte haben keinen Geldwert und können nicht in Geld getauscht oder aus der Familie übertragen werden.
